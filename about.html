About

I’m a childhood experiences educator. I design and lead multimodal, multisensory literacy experiences for young children, where books, play and family life meet.

I began as a Vietnamese Language Arts teacher. When a student showed me a meme and said, “This is how we memorize the poem,” I saw that children already make meaning through images, sound, movement and screens. I have designed learning around that ever since.

Since then I’ve built programs for children and families: Philosophy for Children sessions for more than 100 children across Vietnam, literacy and creative writing courses, a published book of children’s writing, and RURI Books, the children’s bookstore and learning space I co-founded. My Master of Education at the University of Glasgow explored how art, the body and the senses make learning richer.

I now live in Westchester, New York, and I’m a mother to a preschool-aged son. Seeing early childhood through a parent’s eyes has deepened what I care about: children who are curious and heard, surrounded by stories, and families who feel supported. I’m ready to bring my experience in children’s literacy and enrichment into a local childcare center or early learning program.
